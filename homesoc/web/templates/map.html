How each device is drawn

  • C H M L ithe letter inside a device is its most urgent open problem: C {{ 'critical'|sev_word }} (critical), H {{ 'high'|sev_word }} (high), M {{ 'medium'|sev_word }} (medium), L {{ 'low'|sev_word }} (low), i {{ 'info'|sev_word }} (info). Colour repeats the letter, it never carries it alone. No letter means nothing is open.
  • bigger means more relies on it.
  • hollow with a broken outline means it was not seen at Home SOC's last network check (not a live reading); the node's own label reads (offline) after the name, and its tooltip and screen-reader name say so too.
  • square is the internet or an outside service, diamond is the look-up service web blocking runs (the DNS resolver), pentagon is a service a device offers the house, such as printing or casting.
  • a pentagon drawn as a dashed outline is offered but has no confirmed consumers: Home SOC has seen nothing use it, and it will not guess who might. Most home services look like this, because Home SOC cannot see one device using another.
