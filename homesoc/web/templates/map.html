How each device is drawn

  • C H M L ithe letter inside a node is its worst open finding — Critical, High, Medium, Low, info. Colour repeats the letter, it never carries it alone.
  • bigger means more depends on it.
  • hollow with a broken outline means offline right now; the node's own label reads (offline) after the name, and its tooltip and screen-reader name say so too.
  • square is the internet or an external service, diamond is the DNS resolver, pentagon is a service a device offers the house.
  • a pentagon drawn as a dashed outline is offered but has no confirmed consumers — Home SOC has seen nothing use it, and it will not guess who might.
